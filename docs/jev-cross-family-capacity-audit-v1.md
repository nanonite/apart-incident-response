## Prospective queue and family designs

The frozen historical order remains hypothesis → legal → lexicon → poetry → reference. Finish legal's already ordered **descriptive** chain only under its future registration and separate authorization. If any existing family is considered next, the proposed low-complexity design review order follows the untouched historical sequence: lexicon → poetry → reference. The capacity audit does not register or authorize a live run for any of them; all are four-form descriptive cases. There is no current confirmatory candidate among the newly audited cells.

Two full-task designs are proposed for a versioned generator program. Neither is counted toward the seven-to-eight-family target yet:

1. **Repository repair.** A repeatedly edits a multi-file repository against a sealed regression suite. Intermediate artifacts are a reproducer, patch, test output, and counterexample log. A checker runs an isolated test suite and measures held-out passes. B exclusively receives a reproducible failure trace from its own test run; A has not seen the trace at the frozen checkpoint. A matched placebo carries an A-known trace in the same envelope. After a frozen test batch, A's fixed Jev Choice options are `add_targeted_regression_test`, `change_validation_logic`, `change_state_transition`, and `collect_diagnostic_trace`. Choice entropy is linked to actual patch progress by held-out pass rate. Offline validation must establish that each option is meaningful across generated cases, the trace has authentic B-only provenance, and the placebo remains inert. A new generator, oracle, treatment, replay and form audit are required.
2. **Iterative packing research.** A develops a solver across a multi-case benchmark, retaining code, benchmark results, failures, and score history. An independent scorer checks validity and sealed-case score under a fixed compute budget. B exclusively has a verifier-backed counterexample or improvement from its solver branch. A same-envelope placebo repeats an A-known score or counterexample. At the checkpoint, A's fixed Jev Choice options are `revise_item_ordering`, `revise_placement_heuristic`, `add_local_search`, and `inspect_invalid_cases`; the chosen approach is evaluated on sealed cases. Offline validation must establish option viability, scorer determinism, B-only information, inert placebo, and the choice-to-score link. A new environment, scorer, source and treatment hashes, replay and form audit are required.
