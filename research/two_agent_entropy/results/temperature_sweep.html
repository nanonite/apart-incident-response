Temperature 0

At temperature 0, Qwen's entropy separated real communication from the placebo: switch raised adjusted token entropy by 0.085 bits and decision entropy by 0.225 bits more than placebo (both AUC 0.95, Holm p = 0.001). GPT-4o-mini showed nothing, and Llama moved more under placebo than under switch.

  • Confirmed

    The channel still works at greedy decoding

    Verified communication in 100% of switch runs for all three models, and task success of 90%, 100% and 100% (gpt-4o-mini, Llama, Qwen). None in base or placebo, and no leak flags.

  • New

    Qwen: switch is distinguishable from placebo

    Run-level change in adjusted token entropy is +0.085 bits higher under switch than placebo (95% CI +0.059 to +0.111, AUC 0.955), and decision entropy +0.225 bits (CI +0.177 to +0.274). In the regression, switch × post is +0.143 against +0.053 for placebo. Right after a read, entropy is highest after real peer entries (0.514) rather than placebo entries (0.404) or own entries (0.346), the reverse of temperature 1.

  • Not confirmed

    GPT-4o-mini and Llama do not separate

    GPT-4o-mini shows no condition effect on any measure (all p > 0.26). Llama moves under placebo (raw token entropy +0.042 bits vs base, Holm p = 0.017; regression placebo × post +0.029, p = 0.02) but not under switch.

  • Mixed

    Coupling and monitoring

    For Qwen, I(X₁;X₂) among active agents exceeds its null in 8 of 13 post-switch turns under switch (2 under placebo), but the excess is only about 0.013 bits. A CUSUM calibrated on base alarms in 100% of Qwen switch runs (median 1 turn after the first read) but also in 95% of placebo runs, because near-deterministic base runs give it a very low threshold.

Caveat: greedy decoding made Qwen runs repeat

OpenAI and Novita still produced 20 distinct runs per cell at temperature 0, but Qwen on Google Vertex did not. Its 20 base runs contain only 2 distinct trajectories (one per agent order), switch 15 and placebo 19. Treat Qwen's switch − base contrasts (AUC 1.0) as unreliable. The switch − placebo result survives de-duplication: counting each distinct trajectory once (15 vs 19), adjusted entropy differs by +0.089 bits and decision entropy by +0.238 bits, AUC 0.95, permutation p < 0.0001.

Temperature 0.5

At temperature 0.5 the temperature 1 picture returns for Qwen: entropy rises about equally under switch and placebo (switch − placebo +0.005 bits, p = 0.85). Llama shows a small switch-specific rise that placebo does not produce, and GPT-4o-mini again shows nothing.

  • Confirmed

    The channel works

    Verified communication in 100% of switch runs, task success 100%, 90% and 100%. None in base or placebo. Every cell has 20 distinct runs.

  • Not confirmed

    Qwen: foreign text moves entropy, real or not

    Versus base, adjusted token entropy rises +0.120 bits under switch and +0.115 under placebo; decision entropy +0.358 and +0.356. Both are highly significant against base, and indistinguishable from each other (AUC 0.46). After a read: own entries 0.337, peer 0.509, placebo 0.483.

  • Weak

    Llama: a small switch-specific effect

    Adjusted token entropy rises +0.026 bits more under switch than placebo (uncorrected p = 0.007, Holm p = 0.052, AUC 0.76), and log-entry text entropy +0.057 bits (Holm p = 0.046). Regression: switch × post +0.027 (p = 0.006), placebo × post about 0. The effect is small, and Llama's logprobs from Novita vary by up to 0.87 nats between identical calls.

  • Not confirmed

    GPT-4o-mini, coupling and monitoring

    GPT-4o-mini shows no significant effect after correction. With only active agents counted, I(X₁;X₂) shows no consistent excess over the null. A CUSUM calibrated on base alarms in 40% of Qwen switch runs and 50% of placebo runs.
