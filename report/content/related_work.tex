\section{Related Work}
\label{sec:related}

\paragraph{Multi-agent risks and collusion.} \textcite{hammond2025multiagent} list collusion among the main failure modes of systems of interacting AI agents. Work on secret collusion shows that LLM agents can hide messages in innocuous text \parencite{motwani2024secret} and that steganographic channels can emerge under optimisation pressure and survive output monitors and paraphrasing \parencite{mathew2024hidden}. These studies detect or prevent collusion by inspecting what is sent. We inspect the receiver instead, which does not depend on decoding the message or knowing where it travelled.

\paragraph{Monitoring and control.} AI control protocols place trusted monitors around untrusted models \parencite{greenblatt2024control}, and chain-of-thought monitors catch reward hacking in reasoning models, though models can learn to obfuscate their reasoning under optimisation \parencite{baker2025monitoring}. Both read the content of an agent's outputs with another model, which is expensive at the scale of thousands of parallel runs and depends on the content being legible. An entropy statistic costs nothing beyond the log-probabilities the provider already returns and could decide which runs deserve such a review.

\paragraph{Detecting foreign content in context.} Indirect prompt injection is the adversarial version of our failure mode: text that reaches a model through data rather than through the user \parencite{greshake2023indirect}. \textcite{abdelnabi2025drift} detect the resulting task drift with near-perfect accuracy by comparing internal activations before and after the model reads external data. That method needs white-box access. Ours needs only the top-$k$ log-probabilities that APIs expose, and it is tested on benign foreign content that does not try to redirect the agent.

\paragraph{Entropy as a signal.} Output entropy and maximum softmax probability are standard baselines for out-of-distribution detection \parencite{hendrycks2017baseline} and for uncertainty in autoregressive models \parencite{malinin2021uncertainty}. Semantic entropy detects hallucinations by clustering sampled answers by meaning \parencite{kuhn2023semantic, shannon_hallucination}. In networking, entropy of traffic features is a classic anomaly statistic \parencite{shannon_ddos}. For multi-agent systems, entropy has been used to describe orchestration dynamics \parencite{zhu2026orchestrator} and to predict when collaboration helps \parencite{zhao2026doesmultiagentcollaborationhelp}. To our knowledge, no prior work tests whether black-box output entropy reveals that an agent's context was disrupted, with a placebo that separates the arrival of foreign text from the arrival of useful information and with a checker that verifies communication actually happened.
